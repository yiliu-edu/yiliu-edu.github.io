    Approach

    I focus on what learners will need to accomplish afterward, build practice tasks before introducing content, design for learners of all types, and measure the impact.

    Four habits behind every project

    Start from the job afterward

    The learning objectives and the learners' goals will always determine the final product.

    Design practice that connects with real-world scenarios

    Scenarios include realistic characters, options that feature common mistakes with explanations, and chances for learners to recall before reviewing.

    Universal Design for Learning

    Drag-and-drop capabilities, keyboard navigation, low-motion settings, clear instructions and bilingual options.

    Measure and revise

    Surveys before and after completion gauge confidence levels, while first-try accuracy, completeness and success rates show what worked. An AI-assisted workflow tracks decisions made during review for continuous improvement.
