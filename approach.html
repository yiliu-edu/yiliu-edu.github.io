Approach

How I design

I start from what learners need to do afterward, build practice before content, design for every learner, and measure what changed.

Four habits behind every project

Start from the job afterward

What will the learner actually do next week: resolve a ticket, answer a parent, catch an AI mistake? That task sets the objectives.

Design practice, not slides

Realistic people, choices with believable wrong answers, and feedback that explains why. Learners recall before they review.

Build for every learner

Drag or tap, keyboard support, reduced motion, plain language and bilingual options, shaped by my ESL and bilingual teaching.

Measure and revise

Pre/post confidence, first-try accuracy and reach surveys show what worked. With AI workflows, every review decision becomes a rule that improves the next run.
