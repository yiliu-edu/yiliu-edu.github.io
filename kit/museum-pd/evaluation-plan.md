# Evaluation plan and logic model: Discovery Kits professional learning

## Logic model
| Inputs | Activities | Outputs | Short-term outcomes | Long-term outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery Kits, museum educators, grant funding, partner schools | Self-paced module, virtual sessions, Kit Ambassadors meetups | Teachers completing the module; kits borrowed; lessons taught | Teacher confidence with object-based inquiry; multilingual supports used | Students reason from evidence; more classrooms use museum collections |

## Evaluation questions
1. Do teachers leave more confident leading object-based inquiry? (Confidence rated at the start and end of the module)
2. Can teachers separate observations from inferences? (First-try accuracy on the See-Think-Wonder sort)
3. Do teachers teach the lesson? (Check-in two to four weeks after the planned date)
4. Are multilingual students supported? (Languages selected in plans; check-in question)
5. Does student reasoning improve? (Sample of student wonder walls and charts, scored with a 3-level rubric)

## Student reasoning rubric (for shared artifacts)
| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Observations and ideas are mixed; few reasons given |
| 2 | Observations are separate from ideas; some ideas have a reason |
| 3 | Most ideas are backed by an observation; questions lead to next steps |

## Reporting calendar
- Each semester: module completions, kit loans, repeat borrowers, Kit Ambassadors participation
- Yearly (grant report): outcomes above, two teacher stories, sample student work (with consent), what changed in the program as a result
