# Facilitation guide: Look Closely (virtual session, 45 minutes)

For museum educators leading the live session that follows the self-paced module. Up to 30 teachers. Each teacher has the Fossils Discovery Kit or the printable object cards.

## Outcomes
Teachers will:
1. Separate observations from inferences when looking at an object (See-Think-Wonder)
2. Use three facilitation moves: ask for evidence, offer frames in students’ languages, turn disagreement into investigation
3. Leave with a lesson plan they will teach within a month, and one thing to share back

## Run of show
| Time | Activity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Mystery object reveal on camera | Ask for one word in the chat: what do you notice? |
| 5–15 | Slow looking together | Model “What do you see that makes you say that?” with teachers’ chat answers |
| 15–25 | Video clip: a third-grade inquiry | Teachers spot the three moves; debrief in breakout rooms of 4 |
| 25–35 | Adapt the plan | Teachers open the plan from the module; one breakout per grade band |
| 35–42 | Multilingual supports | Share frames in English, Spanish and Mandarin; teachers add frames for other languages in their rooms |
| 42–45 | Commitments | Each teacher posts their date and share-back choice |

## Co-creation
Teachers’ adaptations shared in Kit Ambassadors are reviewed each semester. Strong ones are added to the kit guide, credited to the teacher, with permission.

## Accessibility
Live captions on; object images described aloud; slides shared in advance; breakout instructions posted in the chat.
