# Workshop facilitation guide: AI in Your Course (60 minutes)

For instructional technologists co-facilitating with teaching center staff. Up to 25 faculty, in person or on Zoom. Participants bring one course syllabus.

## Outcomes
By the end, each participant will have:
1. Chosen an AI-use level (0–3) for at least three assignments, based on each assignment’s learning goal
2. Drafted a syllabus statement and a disclosure note ready for Canvas
3. Planned how they would respond to a suspected misuse case without relying on a detector score
4. Picked one assignment to redesign and at least two strategies to try

## Agenda
| Time | Activity | Facilitator notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Opening question: “What did students ask you about AI this term?” | Collect answers in the chat or on sticky notes; name 2–3 patterns |
| 5–15 | The four AI levels | Show one assignment at each level from different disciplines. Stress: the level follows the learning goal |
| 15–30 | Work time: Steps 1–3 of the self-paced kit | Circulate. Common sticking point: one rule for the whole course. Ask “What is this assignment for?” |
| 30–40 | Case discussion: the 87% detector score | Small groups, then debrief. Key points: score is one signal; look at process evidence; talk with the student; fix unclear policy |
| 40–52 | Redesign sprint (Step 5) | Pairs from different disciplines swap ideas; watch the grading-time trade-off |
| 52–60 | Commitments and exit ticket | Each person names one change they’ll make before next term |

## Exit ticket (Kirkpatrick Level 1 and 2)
1. How useful was this workshop for planning your course? (1–5)
2. Which assignment will you change first, and how?
3. What question do you still have about AI tools, detectors or exam software?

## Follow-up
- Week 1 of term: send the statement checklist and a reminder to add disclosure notes to Canvas assignments
- Week 4: offer a 30-minute consultation to review student questions (Level 3: did the statement go live, did student questions drop?)
- End of term: short survey on academic integrity cases and student questions, compared with the previous term (Level 4)

## Partners
Teaching center (pedagogy), library (information literacy and citation of AI), academic integrity office (process and policy), disability services (exam tool accommodations).
