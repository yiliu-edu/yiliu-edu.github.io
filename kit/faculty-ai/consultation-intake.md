# Faculty consultation intake: AI in teaching

Used before a one-on-one consultation so the meeting starts with the course and its goals.

1. Course name, level and enrollment:
2. What should students be able to do by the end of the course?
3. Which assignments are you most concerned about, and why?
4. What have students asked you about AI so far?
5. Have you had a suspected misuse case? What evidence did you have? (No student names.)
6. Which tools do you already use (Canvas features, exam tools, detectors)?
7. What would make this consultation worth your time?

## Consultant checklist
- Start from the learning goal of each assignment before discussing tools
- Name the institution’s current policy and where it leaves choices to the instructor
- Treat detector and exam-tool output as signals, never as the decision
- Leave with: AI level per assignment, a syllabus statement draft, one redesign, and a follow-up date
- Log the question themes (no names) so the team can plan workshops from real needs
