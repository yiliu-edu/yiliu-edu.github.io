# Content standards and partner acceptance criteria

One set of standards for every asset in the catalog, whether built in-house, by a contractor or by an education partner. Used to audit legacy content and to accept new content.

## The six standards
| # | Standard | Meets | Partly | Missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Measurable objectives stated up front | 2–4 objectives with an observable behavior, linked to practice | Topics listed without behaviors | None |
| 2 | Practice that mirrors the job | Decisions in realistic situations with feedback | Recall questions only | None |
| 3 | Assessment that checks the skill | Scenario or performance items aligned to each objective | Some items aligned | None, or recall only |
| 4 | Accurate and current | SME sign-off within 12 months; no retired tools or forms | Minor dated references | Retired tools, forms or rules |
| 5 | Accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA) | Accurate captions, transcript, alt text, contrast, keyboard access | Auto captions or gaps | Not checked |
| 6 | Chunked for busy learners | Pieces under 10 minutes with clear navigation | Long but sectioned | One long recording |

Score: Meets = 2, Partly = 1, Missing = 0. Total out of 12.
- 10–12 Keep (minor edits) · 7–9 Refresh · 3–6 Rebuild · 0–2 Retire, or rebuild only if demand is high

## Partner acceptance
A partner course is accepted when it:
1. Scores 9 or higher, with no “Missing” on standards 4 or 5
2. Passes the LMS readiness checklist below in the sandbox
3. Names an owner and a review date, and agrees to update within 30 days when rules or forms change
4. Shares learner-level data in the agreed format (SCORM 2004 completion and score, xAPI for practice)

## LMS readiness checklist
- Captions edited for accuracy; transcript attached
- Alt text on meaningful images; decorative images marked
- Text contrast at WCAG 2.1 AA; all interactions work by keyboard; focus order checked
- Published as SCORM 2004 (or xAPI package) and tested in the LMS sandbox: launch, resume, completion, score
- xAPI statements for practice decisions reach the learning record store
- Metadata: title, description, objectives, audience, duration, owner, version, review date, keywords
- Brand: approved template, logo use and color tokens
- SME sign-off recorded

## Governance
- Every asset has one owner and a review date (12 months by default; sooner when rules, forms or tools change)
- Quarterly catalog report: views, completion, rating, and the practice items most often missed
- Changes are versioned (major.minor) with a one-line change note
