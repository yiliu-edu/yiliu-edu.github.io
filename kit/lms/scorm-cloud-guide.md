# Test the SCORM package in SCORM Cloud

SCORM Cloud is Rustici Software’s testing LMS. The free Trial plan has no time limit and allows 3 courses and 10 active registrations.

1. Create a Trial account at cloud.scorm.com.
2. In the Library, add new content and upload `right-first-time-scorm2004.zip` as a SCORM package.
3. Open the course and choose Launch. Take the course once, and miss label 1 on your first try so the report shows an incorrect interaction.
4. Close the course window, then open the course in the Library and view your registration’s report.
5. Check and screenshot:
   - Completion: completed
   - Success: passed (80% or higher) or failed
   - Score: scaled score, raw score out of 5
   - Interactions: label-1 to label-4 and deviation-record, with learner responses and results
6. Launch again before finishing to test resume: the course should open on the slide where you left off.

What to say in an interview: “I published the course as a SCORM 2004 package, tested completion, scoring, interactions and bookmarking in SCORM Cloud, and checked the registration report.”
