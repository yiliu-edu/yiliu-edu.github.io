## Logging
Log each issue in the QC tracker: ID, screen, language, issue, the storyboard line it breaks, severity, status.

Severity
- High: wrong content or audio a student would learn from (tone marks, wrong audio, wrong word)
- Medium: accessibility or feedback gaps
- Low: visual polish that doesn’t affect learning
