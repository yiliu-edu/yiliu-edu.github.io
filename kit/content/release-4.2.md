# Kitebird Projects 4.2 release notes (November 4, 2026)

- The Timeline view is now called Roadmap, and it lives under the new Plan tab.
- You can now assign a group as an approver. Any one member of the group can approve.
- Guest access is now off by default for new workspaces. Admins can turn it on in Settings > Security.
- CSV export has moved from Reports > Export to the Share menu on every report. You can also export to PDF.
- Projects with more than 5,000 tasks now load up to 40% faster.

Kitebird Projects is a fictional product created for this portfolio demo.
