# Training needs analysis: label inspection at branches

## Request
Quality leadership asked for “labeling training” after an increase in label-related holds found downstream, after product had left the branch.

## What we looked at
- Twelve months of label nonconformances by type and where they were caught (illustrative categories for this portfolio case)
- Interviews with inspectors, labeling operators and two branch QA leads
- Observation of inspection at the station

## Findings (Gilbert’s Behavior Engineering Model)
| Cell | Finding | Solution | Training? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Information | Inspectors weren’t told which error types were rising | Monthly one-line summary of top error types at each station | No |
| Resources | Work order and label were on different screens; no check card | Station check card; work order printed beside the label | No |
| Incentives | Speed was tracked, catches weren’t | Recognize catches in shift huddles; track right-first-time | No |
| Knowledge | Few inspectors used the UDI to cross-check lot and date | Scenario practice: inspect labels against work orders, with UDI cross-checks | Yes |
| Capacity | Inspection at the end of long shifts | Rotate inspection duty; one-minute micro-cases in place of long refreshers | Partly |
| Motives | Holding product felt like slowing the branch down | Show the cost of a missed error vs. a hold in the opening story | Partly |

## Recommendation
A 15-minute scenario-based module, a station check card, spaced micro-cases (days 2, 7 and 30), and two non-training changes (monthly error summary, catch recognition). Owners and dates agreed with the branch QA leads.

## Objectives
1. Given a label and its work order, an inspector will identify every nonconforming field, with no misses, in 4 of 4 practice labels.
2. Given an inspection result, an inspector will choose release or hold according to the rule “any mismatch is held”.
3. Given a held lot, an inspector will select or write a deviation record that is attributable, contemporaneous, specific and accurate.
