# Reinforcement and evaluation plan: Right First Time

## Spaced reinforcement
| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Scenario-based module | 15 min |
| Day 2 | One micro-case on the shift tablet | 1 min |
| Day 7 | Micro-case with a new error type (UDI mismatch) | 1 min |
| Day 30 | Three mixed cases and a supervisor observation | 5 min |
| Ongoing | Station check card; monthly top-error summary | — |

## Evaluation (Kirkpatrick)
| Level | Measure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Reaction | Confidence before and after; usefulness of the check card | Module and Day 30 pulse |
| 2 Learning | Labels right on the first try; record and correction choices; micro-case accuracy | Module and micro-case data |
| 3 Behavior | Supervisor observation with the check card at Day 30; record quality in a monthly sample | Supervisor checklist; QA record review |
| 4 Results | Label nonconformances caught at the branch vs. found downstream; holds released within target time | Quality management system, quarterly |

## L&D quality standard for this asset
- Objectives stated and tied to practice and assessment
- Content reviewed by a Quality SME against current SOPs and approved label specifications
- Accessible: keyboard operable, contrast checked, all label content readable as text
- Owner and review date; reviewed when label specifications or SOPs change
