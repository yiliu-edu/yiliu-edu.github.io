About

Teacher first, designer by habit

I'm Yi Liu (Louise), a learning experience designer and bilingual educator based in Chicago.

My story

I've taught in K–12 classrooms, IB programs and university tutorials across Hong Kong, Shanghai and Chicago. Along the way I kept building things for learners and for the people who teach them: lesson sequences, staff onboarding, a school website, coding courses, and now interactive practice tools for a Google-partnered program at Digital4Good.

Right now I lead curriculum for Meet LEO (online safety for families) and Meet LEA (AI literacy for educators). I also built the program's data system: a pre-webinar survey that estimates how many families each attendee will reach, and a post-webinar practice site that shows what trainers actually remember.

I'm finishing an MSEd in Elementary Education at Northwestern with ESL and Bilingual endorsements, and I student-teach 2nd grade in Chicago Public Schools.

AI in my practice

I design with AI, and I teach others to use it well

AI is part of how I build, not a side skill. I use it to prototype faster, to keep content accurate, and to help educators use it responsibly, always with a person reviewing what goes out.

Build with Claude

I prototype interactive learning with Claude and code: scenario practice, onboarding paths and simulations that learners can try the same week.

Design AI workflows

I write reusable skills and prompts, structure content so AI can work with it, and set review rules for accuracy and accessibility. See Release-Ready Learning Content.

Teach AI literacy

I develop Meet LEA, AI literacy training for educators in a Google partnership, and I've introduced K–12 students to AI with tools like Teachable Machine.

Create content faster

I use Gemini for research and drafts, Canva for visuals and CapCut for short learning videos, then edit everything to my own standard.
