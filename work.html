    My process while working with stakeholders:

    Needs Analysis

    Find the performance gap behind the request. I interview stakeholders, observe the work and sort causes with Gilbert's Behavior Engineering Model, so training is only built where training is the fix.

    Design

    Write measurable objectives, plan backward from the evidence of success, and choose the lightest format that changes the behavior: a job aid, a scenario, a simulation or a workshop.

    Develop

    Build realistic practice with feedback for every choice, in plain language and accessible by default. I prototype quickly with HTML and AI tools, then refine with real learners.

    Implement

    Launch with the people who deliver it: facilitator guides, train-the-trainer sessions and clear ownership, so the learning keeps working after hand-off.

    Evaluate

    Agree on Kirkpatrick-level measures before launch, collect first-try accuracy and confidence data, and fold every finding back into the next version.
