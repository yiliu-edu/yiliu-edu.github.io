Pricing Conversation with Priya

Scenario
Resources Content standards xAPI tracking plan Full modernization workspace

A branching scenario built in HTML on the same model as an Articulate Storyline 360 project: slides, a feedback layer, character states, a trust variable and triggers. The Storyline build guide in the sales onboarding project shows how each part maps to Storyline. Priya and Lakeshore Realty Learning are fictional, and the pricing guidance is general; Priya’s voice is AI-generated with Kokoro, an open-source text-to-speech model. Designed and developed by Yi Liu, 2026.
